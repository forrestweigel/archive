# Archive release history

## Alpha Revision 2

- Renamed Failed Search to Discovery; its effect is unchanged.
- Added Research: once during each of your turns, as a sorcery, pay 4 mana of any type to put the top card of your Archive into your hand. Research and Exchange have separate once-per-turn limits.

## Alpha Revision 1

- Increased the starting Library to 50 cards, making the standard split 50 Library cards and 49 Archive cards.
- Clarified that all remaining deck cards form the Archive, so it may not contain exactly 49 cards.

Initial public rules release.
