# Archive release history

## Alpha Revision 4

- Changed Discovery to reveal Archive cards until the first card that qualifies for the failed Library search. You must take that card where the search specifies, following its instructions for that card. Put the other revealed cards on the bottom in a random order; if none qualify, you get no card.

## Alpha Revision 3

- Changed Research from taking the top Archive card into hand to looking at it and choosing whether to leave it on top or put it on the bottom. Reduced its cost from 4 to 3 mana of any type; it remains once during each of your turns, as a sorcery.

## Alpha Revision 2

- Renamed Failed Search to Discovery; its effect is unchanged.
- Added Research: once during each of your turns, as a sorcery, pay 4 mana of any type to put the top card of your Archive into your hand. Research and Exchange have separate once-per-turn limits.

## Alpha Revision 1

- Increased the starting Library to 50 cards, making the standard split 50 Library cards and 49 Archive cards.
- Clarified that all remaining deck cards form the Archive, so it may not contain exactly 49 cards.

Initial public rules release.
